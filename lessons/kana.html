Kana chart

Tap any character to hear it.

How to read the chart: Each row is a consonant (k, s, t…) and each column is a vowel: a, i, u, e, o. Read across the row, for example か き く け こ = ka ki ku ke ko. Hiragana is used for native words and grammar; katakana is used for foreign words such as フィードバック. Both sets make the same sounds. A blank cell means that sound isn't used.
Two small marks change the sound: ゛ (dakuten) makes k→g, s→z, t→d and h→b, and ゜ (handakuten) makes h→p. A small ゃ, ゅ or ょ after an i-column kana blends the two, for example き+ゃ = きゃ (kya).
