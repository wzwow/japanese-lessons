Learning plan
Beginner to advanced: 5 stages from N5 to N1, week by week
Kana chart
Hiragana & katakana with audio
Lesson 1: First meetings with clients
Greetings and polite phrases for client meetings
Lesson 2: Design review phrases
Listen, repeat, and get speech feedback
Lesson 3: Everyday phrases
Everyday greetings and phrases for work and daily life
Lesson 4: Self-introduction
Your name, where you're from, and what you do: X は Y です
Lesson 5: Business cards & polite phrases
Exchange business cards, greet clients and apologize politely: 〜ます / 〜ございます
Lesson 6: Names & さん
Ask for a name, introduce someone, ask for a client, and leave politely: name + さん
Lesson 7: Week 1 review
Warm-up + quiz checkpoint for self-intro, business cards, and names with さん
Lesson 8: Talking about your work
Possession with の, plus asking about jobs: 〜ですか
Lesson 9: Me too — saying “also” with も
Agree, add one more item, and say “both A and B”: X も Y です
Lesson 10: Asking “what?” with 何 (なん)
Ask what something is, what it’s for, and how to say it in Japanese: X は何ですか
Lesson 11: Who you are at work — company, role & team with の
Say which company you’re with, your role, what you’re in charge of, and correct a wrong guess: A の B です
Lesson 12: Whose is it? — 誰の and の on its own
Ask whose design something is and say it’s yours, your team’s or from a previous job: 誰のですか / 私のです
Lesson 13: Who’s in charge? — roles, specialties & A or B questions
Ask who’s in charge, say your specialty, react with そうですか and ask “A or B?”: ご担当はどなたですか / ロゴですか、ウェブサイトですか
Lesson 14: Week 2 review
Warm-up + quiz checkpoint for の, も, 何 / 誰の questions, roles & A or B questions
Lesson 15: In the studio — pointing
これ / それ / あれ / どれ: それは何ですか, あれはモックアップですか, どれが…ですか
Lesson 16: This design, that file — この / その / あの / どの
この / その / あの / どの + noun: このデザインはどうですか, そのファイルは最新ですか, どの色がいいですか
Lesson 17: That isn't the logo — negatives
〜じゃありません / 〜ではありません: あれはロゴじゃありません, これは最終版じゃありません, 私の担当ではありません
